# 005 — Recon and EW Out of Scope

**Status:** Accepted
**Date:** 2026-04-13

## Context
Early variant list included reconnaissance/sensor and electronic warfare variants. Both were evaluated against the chassis concept.

## Decision
Both reconnaissance and EW are out of scope for this chassis. They should be separate programs.

### Reconnaissance/Sensor
A drone mothership concept was considered — the vehicle deploys expendable sensor nodes and manages a mix of recon drones (quadcopters for close-in, fixed-wing for area coverage). However, the internal volume needed for drone storage, launch/recovery, maintenance, and recharging is incompatible with a 19-tonne armored fighting chassis.

### Electronic Warfare
Reviewed existing EW vehicles (Fuchs ELoPa with 19m mast, Dutch Bushmaster EW, Czech STARKOM). EW platforms operate behind the lines with tall antenna masts and broadcast large electromagnetic signatures. The Pangolin is a frontline fighting vehicle that closes with the enemy — fundamentally different operational profile.

## Consequences
- Pangolin platoon depends on external assets for EW and wide-area reconnaissance
- Recon and EW become requirements for companion programs
