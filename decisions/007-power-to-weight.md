# 007 — Power-to-Weight Ratio Target

**Status:** Accepted
**Date:** 2026-04-13

## Context
Modern wheeled AFVs range from 14.5 hp/tonne (Boxer) to 23.4 hp/tonne (LAV-25), with most clustering around 17-22 hp/tonne.

## Decision
Target 25 bhp/tonne — best in class for wheeled AFVs.

## Rationale
- Wide-area operations and dispersed movement demand high agility
- Enables rapid repositioning on a sensor-saturated battlefield where staying still is lethal
- At this ratio the vehicle outpaces anything in its class

## Consequences
- Engine selection is driven by final vehicle weight (e.g. 375 hp at 15t, 475 hp at 19t)
- Higher power density may require careful thermal management
- Fuel consumption and range will need to be balanced against power output
