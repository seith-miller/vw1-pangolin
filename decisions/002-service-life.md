# 002 — Service Life 2030-2050

**Status:** Accepted
**Date:** 2026-04-13

## Context
Military vehicles typically serve 30-40+ years (M113 since 1960s, Bradley since 1980s, Leopard 2 since 1979). A shorter design horizon allows tighter optimization for near-term threats and technology.

## Decision
- Initial operating capability: 2030
- Designed relevance through: 2050
- 20-year service window

## Rationale
A 20-year window means designing for the threats and technology of the next two decades rather than trying to be all things forever. AI and autonomy will mature significantly over this period — the vehicle should be designed to take advantage of that trajectory.

## Consequences
- Can make bolder technology choices knowing the platform won't need to last 50 years
- Autonomy roadmap: starts crewed with AI assist (2030), crew remains but AI capabilities grow through service life
