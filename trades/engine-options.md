# Trade Study — Engine Options

**Status:** Open
**Date:** 2026-04-13
**Related:** Target 25 bhp/tonne ([decision 007](../decisions/007-power-to-weight.md))

## Requirement
Engine must deliver 25 bhp/tonne at final vehicle combat weight. For reference:
- 15t vehicle = 375 hp
- 17t vehicle = 425 hp
- 19t vehicle = 475 hp

Military requirements: multi-fuel capable, hardened electronics, field-serviceable.

## Candidates

| Engine | HP | Displacement | Weight | Notes |
|--------|-----|-------------|--------|-------|
| Caterpillar C9 | 375 | 8.8L | 864 kg | Compact, light |
| Caterpillar C13 | 385-536 | 12.5L | 1,180 kg | Wide hp range |
| Cummins ISX15 | 400-600 | 14.9L | — | Scalable, headroom |
| Deutz TCD 9.0 | 402 | 9.0L | — | 4-cylinder, compact |
| Scania DC13 | 400-450 | 13L | — | Proven platform |

## Notes
- All candidates are commercial engines — would need militarized variants
- Cat C9 and Deutz TCD 9.0 are most weight-conscious
- Cummins ISX15 gives most headroom if vehicle grows
- Final selection depends on vehicle combat weight
